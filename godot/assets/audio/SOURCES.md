# Audio sources

- `countryside.mp3`: “Countryside” (sound 0097), recorded by Joseph Sardin,
  [BigSoundBank](https://bigsoundbank.com/countryside-s0097.html), CC0 1.0.
  Used as a quiet outdoor ambience; playback fades according to known terrain and camera zoom.

## Synthesized sounds (`synth/`)

Every file in `synth/` is original procedural sound design generated by
[`tools/synth-audio.mjs`](../../../tools/synth-audio.mjs) from noise, oscillators and filters. No
third-party recordings are involved; the files are released with the project under CC0 1.0. Re-run
`node tools/synth-audio.mjs` to regenerate them deterministically (ffmpeg encodes Ogg Vorbis).

- Ambience loops: `amb_wind`, `amb_rain`, `amb_water`, `amb_city`, `amb_industry`,
  `amb_construction`, `amb_battle`.
- Combat one-shots: `sfx_rifle`, `sfx_mg`, `sfx_autocannon`, `sfx_cannon`, `sfx_mortar`,
  `sfx_artillery`, `sfx_rocket`, `sfx_explosion`, `sfx_explosion_big`, `sfx_collapse`.

`fire.wav`, `impact.wav` and `kill.wav` are earlier project-made placeholders kept as fallbacks.
