            Menu

            Shortcuts: right-click move (aircraft: patrol; enemy: strike; own airfield: return), right-click an enemy to attack (fire is spread over nearby threats; Ctrl/Cmd+right-click focus fire), right-drag to draw a deployment line (shorter = denser; Tab reverses the facing while dragging, Esc cancels), WASD or arrows pan, Ctrl/Cmd+A attack-move, H hold, X withdraw, P pause (single player), T research, B build (Defenses → Trench Line to dig in), / search, Shift+right-click queue a waypoint, Alt+right-click attack-move, F cycle formation (found a city with colonists), R road planner, G form army, Ctrl+1–9 save group / 1–9 recall (twice to centre), double-click a unit for all of its type on screen (Ctrl/Cmd for the whole map), Alt-drag to box-select buildings, Shift-click to add a building, double-click a building for all of that type on screen (then train in total or per building, or set repeat production), Space centre, L cycle lenses, K map key, trackpad or wheel to pan, pinch or +/- to zoom.

            Spectating: [ and ] follow the previous/next empire, P pause, , and . slower/faster, V toggles the followed empire's fog. WASD and arrows pan.

            Sound

            You only hear what you can see: fighting under the fog stays silent.
