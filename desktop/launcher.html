Desktop edition

This computer

Play against the AI or open the combat sandbox. Runs the game server on your machine; matches are saved on your disk and work offline.

Online

Connect to game.liamhosfeld.com for multiplayer matches with anyone, in the browser or on the desktop.

Steam

Host a match for your Steam friends: your computer runs the server and friends connect through Steam, with no ports to open. Uses Steam's public test app (Spacewar) while the game has no store page.

    Hosting opens the Steam invite dialog. Friends can also accept the invite from Steam chat, and the game joins automatically.
